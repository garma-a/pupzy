## Rehab and Fayrouz

Both are real Port Said-region areas. Rehab is a housing area in El Dawahy, corroborated by the official [ITDA commercial register](https://www.itda.gov.eg/CRM/885/CRA78.pdf), which records an address in مساكن الرحاب in PortSaid, and the governorate indexed homepage development announcement. No exact usable point was found, so Rehab remains unresolved.

Fayrouz is the BorFouad social-housing district, explicitly discussed by the [PortSaid governorate](https://portsaid.gov.eg/News/details/109694). Direct access returns an accessibility shell; its original indexed first-party text is preserved in web-search-1, not claimed as a successful full-page extraction. The proposed approximate locality point is the recorded OSM apartment الفيروز١٥٣ in ثانبورفؤاد, not the unrelated Ismailia street.

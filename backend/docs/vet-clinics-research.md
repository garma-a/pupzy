# Egypt Vet Clinic web research — stage 2

Access date: 2026-11-12. Clinic records are written manually after reading their specific source pages. Search results and category pages supply discovery leads; they are not copied into the release. Access dates and copyright years are not dates of operation. No Google Maps requests, pins or Places API; no Nominatim requests in this research pass.

## Alexandria

The main review produced **89 input clinic records**, plus a separate reviewer's 26 records. Final catalog assignment, duplicate merges and active counts are in `vet-clinics-progress.md`. The main ledger contains 13 held groups and five exclusion groups; some groups contain several unresolved directory leads. These group counts should not be presented as exact counts of independent clinics.

Discovery covered 91 Alexandria-labelled Welpstar category entries across pages 1–13, including repeats, existing records and non-clinics. Pages 14–17 returned HTTP 500. Three Dalili Medical category pages supplied 54 unique doctor/clinic page URLs. Each unmatched selected entry was fetched separately and reviewed. Additional English and Arabic searches covered Smouha, Sidi Bishr, Miami, Kafr Abdo, Stanley, Asafra, Maamoura, Abu Qir and San Stefano. The extra reviewer covered west Alexandria and central areas.

Distinct retained source pages in the main review: animalplanet.clinic: 1, dalil140.com: 7, sites.google.com: 1, superpetegy.com: 1, welpstar.com: 39, www.dalilimedical.com: 39, www.findglocal.com: 2, www.findinegypt.com: 1, www.voofla.com: 4.

Primary clinic websites and individual Facebook pages were attempted. Facebook returned online-fetch throttling. DrVet, PetsHub and Sporting clinic domains returned 502 or were unavailable. Current Animal Planet and Super Pet contact pages worked. `infoeg.com` was robots-blocked. Initial EVC homepage requests timed out, but `www.evcindex.com` category and individual pages worked for the extra reviewer; EVC is not generally blocked. Yellow Pages was not accessed or bypassed.

Decisions for the lead:

- Distinct sourced chain branches remain separate even when phones or pages are shared. Riyada/Victoria entries for Dr. Ehab Saad describe the same two addresses and were merged per address. Wesam Tawfik's Sidi Bishr branch and El Morshedy share both phone and address and retain both sources in one clinic.
- Animal Club retains only its physical clinical services, which the detailed entry explicitly describes alongside pharmacy and farm services. Pharmacy-only and trade businesses were excluded.
- Generic Arabic descriptors are translated. Only brands are transliterated. Origins record reviewer-authored text and mixed translations/transliterations.
- `Opened All Days`, an emergency label, a shop website available 24/7 and 24-hour phone contact are insufficient for `is24Hours`. Only explicit clinic/emergency operation claims set the flag.
- Dr. Vet (5 Khalil El Khayyat versus older 9 Kordahi), Pets Haven (28 El Qods versus older 27 Ibrahim Sherif), Amazon (280 versus older 264 Gamal Abdel Nasser) and Sidi Bishr Clinic (57 versus older 5 Mosque Street) retain one practice each, preferring the detailed or dated operation source. Lead spot-checks should verify these address conflicts.
- Royal, Fluffy Pets 2, Dr. Green and Wingat remain held for conflicting addresses, ambiguous identity or unconfirmed locality; their details are in `research-alexandria-ledger.json`. This is broad initial research with explicit unresolved leads, not a claim that every operating business has been found.

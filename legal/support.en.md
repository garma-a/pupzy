## Common questions

### How do I report a post, comment or account?

Open the post, comment or profile, tap the **⋯** menu and choose **Report**.
Reports are reviewed by our team, and we aim to act on content that breaks our
rules within 24 hours.

### How do I block someone?

Tap the **⋯** menu on their post, comment or profile and choose **Block**. You
and the blocked person won't see each other's content or be able to interact.
You can unblock people from your Profile.

### How do I turn notifications on or off?

In the app, open **Profile** and tap **Notifications**. You can also turn them off
in your phone's settings.

### How do I delete my account?

See [Delete your account]({{delete_account_url}}).

### Someone is asking me for money

Never pay in advance to someone you haven't verified. Adoption posts must not
ask for a price for the animal, and Pupzy never asks you for payment. Report the
account and block it.

### I found a pet that someone reported lost

Before handing the animal over, check that the person is really its owner. For
example, ask for photos of them with the pet or for its vaccination record.
Meet in a public place.

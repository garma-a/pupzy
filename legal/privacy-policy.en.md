## 1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how **{{legal_entity_name}}** ("**Pupzy**", "**we**",
"**us**"), {{registered_address}}, collects, uses, shares and protects personal
data when you use the Pupzy mobile apps, website and related services (the
"**Service**"). We are responsible for your personal data under this policy.

If you have questions, contact us at {{support_email}}.

## 2. The data we collect

### 2.1 Data you give us

- **Account details:** your name (in English and/or Arabic), email address, phone
  number, home city, language and notification preference, and profile photo
  if you add one.
- **Sign-in details:** when you sign in with email, Google or Apple, the sign-in
  provider confirms your identity and shares your email and, where available,
  your name and profile photo. We never receive your Google or Apple password.
- **Posts:** the content of Rescue, Lost & Found, Adoption, Marketplace and Find
  a Mate posts, including photos, descriptions, animal details, prices and
  approximate area or landmark.
- **Discussions:** comments, replies, photos in Rescue comments, and Raise
  signals.
- **Requests and applications:** contact request messages, and adoption
  application answers such as your living situation, whether you have outdoor
  space, other pets or children at home, time at home, pet experience and why
  you want to adopt.
- **Safety reports:** reports you file about posts, comments or accounts, and the
  accounts you block.
- **Support messages** you send us.

### 2.2 Data collected when you use the Service

- **Location:** if you allow it, the app uses your device's location when you
  set up your profile (to suggest your city), when you create a post (to
  record where it is), and to show posts near you. See section 5.
- **Device and technical data:** a push notification token and device platform
  (Android or iOS) so we can send notifications, and technical data such as
  your IP address and request logs, which our servers record for security and
  troubleshooting.
- **Activity data:** your saves, the posts you view, the time you accepted our
  Terms and which version, and your notifications.

### 2.3 What we do **not** collect

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, we do not show ads, and we do
not collect your contacts, messages outside Pupzy, or payment card details.
Pupzy does not process payments.

## 3. How we use your data

We use your data to:

- create and run your account and let you sign in;
- publish your posts, comments and replies and show them to other users;
- show posts near you and in your city, and calculate distances;
- connect people: share contact details when you approve a contact request (see
  section 4.1), and deliver adoption applications to post owners;
- send you notifications about activity on your posts and account;
- keep the community safe: review reports, detect and remove objectionable or
  illegal content, enforce our Terms, prevent fraud and abuse, and suspend
  accounts;
- record your acceptance of our Terms;
- respond to your support requests;
- maintain, secure and improve the Service; and
- comply with the law and with requests from competent authorities.

We process your data because it is necessary to provide the Service you asked
for under our Terms, with your consent (for example, for location and
notifications, which you can withdraw at any time), to keep the Service and its
users safe, and to comply with our legal obligations.

## 4. Who can see or receive your data

### 4.1 Other users

- **Public to other users:** your name and profile photo, your posts and their
  photos, your comments and replies, and your city.
- **Location of posts:** the location of **Rescue** and **Lost & Found** posts
  is shown on the post, because it is needed to find the animal. For Adoption,
  Marketplace and Find a Mate posts, we only show the city and area you enter,
  and use the location to calculate distance.
- **Your phone number** is never shown publicly. It is shared as a WhatsApp
  contact only:
  - with a person whose contact request you **approve**, or whose request to you
    is approved;
  - with an adoption applicant whose application you approve, or with the owner
    when your application is approved; and
  - with buyers, if you create a **Marketplace** listing.
- **Adoption applications** are visible only to the owner of the post you apply
  to.
- **Blocked users** cannot see your content, and you cannot see theirs.

### 4.2 Service providers

We use trusted providers that process data on our behalf, under contracts and
only for the purposes above:

- **Google Firebase** (Google LLC) for sign-in and push notifications;
- **Apple** (Sign in with Apple and Apple Push Notification service on iOS);
- **Cloudflare** for storing and delivering photos;
- **{{hosting_provider}}** for hosting our servers and database.

### 4.3 Other disclosures

We may disclose data if required by law, a court order or a competent
authority; to protect the safety of people or animals; to investigate fraud or
abuse; or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of the Service, subject to
this policy.

**We do not sell your personal data** and we do not share it for advertising.

## 5. Location

- Location access is optional. You can decline it and still use the Service by
  choosing your city manually.
- When you create a post, the app records the device's location at that time.
- You can turn off location access at any time in your phone's settings.
- Photo location data (EXIF) is removed from photos before they are uploaded.

## 6. International transfers

Some of our providers store or process data outside Egypt, for example in the
European Union or the United States. When we transfer data, we take steps
required by applicable law to protect it.

## 7. How long we keep your data

- We keep your account data for as long as your account exists.
- Adoption and Marketplace posts may expire after a period of inactivity. You
  can renew or delete them.
- When you **delete your account**, we remove your account, posts, photos and
  personal information. Some data may remain for up to **30 days** before it is
  permanently deleted.
- We may keep limited records for longer where the law requires it, or where
  it is needed to resolve disputes, prevent fraud or repeat abuse, or enforce
  our Terms. For example, we may keep a record of a ban and of moderation
  decisions.
- Server logs are kept for a limited period for security.

## 8. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit (HTTPS), encryption of phone
numbers at rest, access controls, and by removing location metadata from
photos. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password and
keep your device secure.

## 9. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, including Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law, you
can:

- **access** your data and **correct** it (most of it directly in your profile);
- **delete** your account and data (Profile → Delete Account, or see
  {{delete_account_url}});
- **withdraw consent** for location or notifications in the app or in your
  phone's settings;
- **object to** or ask us to **restrict** certain processing; and
- **receive a copy** of your data.

To exercise these rights, email {{support_email}} from the address linked to your
account. We will respond within the time required by law. You may also complain
to the competent data protection authority in Egypt.

## 10. Children

Pupzy is only for people aged **18 and over**. We do not knowingly collect data
from children. If you believe a child is using Pupzy, contact us at
{{safety_email}} and we will delete the account.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will publish the new version with a new date and,
for significant changes, tell you in the app.

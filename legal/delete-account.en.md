# Delete your Pupzy account

You can delete your Pupzy account and its data at any time.

---

## Delete it in the app

1. Open the Pupzy app and sign in.
2. Tap your profile picture at the top of the screen to open **Profile**.
3. Scroll down and tap **Delete Account**.
4. Read what will be deleted, confirm, and follow the steps on screen.

## Can't use the app?

Email **{{support_email}}** from the email address linked to your Pupzy account,
with the subject **"Delete my Pupzy account"**. Include the name on your account.

[Email a deletion request](mailto:{{support_email}}?subject=Delete%20my%20Pupzy%20account)

To protect your account, we may ask you to confirm the request before we delete
anything. We complete confirmed requests within 30 days.

## What is deleted

- Your account and profile: name, email, phone number, city and profile photo.
- All your posts and their photos, including unresolved Rescue and Lost & Found
  posts.
- Your comments, replies, contact requests and adoption applications.
- Your saved posts, notifications and device registrations for notifications.

## What may be kept, and for how long

- Some data may remain in our systems for up to **30 days** before it is
  permanently deleted.
- We may keep limited records for longer where the law requires it, or to
  resolve disputes, prevent fraud or repeat abuse, and enforce our Terms. For
  example, we may keep a record that an account was banned, and moderation
  decisions.
- Messages or contact details that you already shared with other people outside
  Pupzy, for example on WhatsApp, are not under our control.

Deletion is permanent. A deleted account and its content can't be restored. If
you sign up again later, you start with a new, empty account.

For more details, see our [Privacy Policy]({{privacy_url}}).

# Pupzy Child Safety Standards

**Last updated:** 27 September 2026

---

Pupzy has **zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)**,
including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion,
trafficking, or any content or behaviour that sexualises or endangers
children.

## Our standards

- Pupzy is only for people aged **18 and over**. Accounts we believe belong to
  children are removed.
- Any content or behaviour that sexualises, exploits or endangers children is
  prohibited by our [Terms of Service]({{terms_url}}). It is removed as soon as we
  become aware of it, and the account responsible is permanently banned.
- We report child sexual abuse material and child exploitation to the competent
  authorities in Egypt and, where applicable, to the relevant international
  bodies, and we cooperate with law enforcement.
- We preserve relevant information when the law requires it to support
  investigations.

## How to report

- **In the app:** open the post, comment or profile, tap the **⋯** menu and choose
  **Report**. Reports are reviewed by our team.
